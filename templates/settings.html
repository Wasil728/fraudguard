How the Threshold Knob Works

In machine learning for fraud detection, models output a continuous probability $P(\text{fraud}) \in [0, 1]$, mapped to a Risk Score from 0 to 100.

If you lower the threshold (e.g. 25%):
  • Recall increases: Catches nearly 100% of fraud attacks.
  • Precision drops: Innocent users face OTP challenges or temporary holds.
If you raise the threshold (e.g. 80%):
  • Precision increases: Every raised alarm is almost guaranteed fraud.
  • Recall drops: Sneaky low-value test attacks may slip through unnoticed.
