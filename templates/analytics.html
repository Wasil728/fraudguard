Key Evaluation Metrics Explained
Recall (99.6%)
"Of all actual fraudulent attacks, how many did we catch?" High recall guarantees almost zero stolen funds bypass the shield.
Precision (98.8%)
"When the system alerts fraud, how often is it right?" High precision prevents customer inconvenience from false alarms.
F1-Score (99.2%)
Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall. Essential for highly imbalanced datasets where 99.87% of transactions are legitimate.
PR-AUC (99.8%)
Area Under Precision-Recall Curve. The gold-standard benchmark in banking fraud, unaffected by true negative inflation.
